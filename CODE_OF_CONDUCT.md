# Cozy Watch Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Cozy Watch contributors and maintainers are expected to make participation welcoming, respectful, and constructive. Examples of positive behavior include giving specific technical feedback, assuming good intent while addressing impact, respecting privacy, and helping newcomers contribute safely.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, publishing another person's private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Scope

This code applies in Cozy Watch repositories, issue trackers, reviews, discussions, and other spaces where someone is representing the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to [tiago@cozywatch.com](mailto:tiago@cozywatch.com). Include links or context that help the maintainer understand what happened, but do not send credentials or unrelated personal information.

The maintainer may remove content, issue a warning, restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant depending on the seriousness and pattern of behavior. Reports will be handled as privately as reasonably possible, while recognizing that an investigation may require limited disclosure. Retaliation against a reporter or participant in an investigation is prohibited.

Questions about security vulnerabilities belong in the private process described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
