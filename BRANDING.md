# Cozy Watch Branding Policy

This policy covers the Cozy Watch name, names that are confusingly similar, logos, icons, artwork, domain names, and other brand identifiers (the **Cozy Watch Marks**). The MIT License covers source code; it does not grant trademark rights or permission to imply endorsement.

## Permitted uses

You may:

- refer to Cozy Watch factually in articles, reviews, tutorials, package metadata, and compatibility documentation;
- link to the official repository or website;
- say that a project is “based on Cozy Watch” when that statement is accurate; and
- use unmodified Cozy Watch marks when pointing users to the unmodified official project, provided the presentation does not suggest sponsorship or endorsement.

Use the marks exactly as provided where possible. Keep logos and icons legible, do not distort them, and do not combine them with another mark into a new logo.

## Forks and modified builds

Forks and modified builds must use a distinct product name, application identifier, icons, and visual identity when distributed as their own product. A clear notice such as “An unofficial fork of Cozy Watch” is welcome, but do not present a modified build as an official Cozy Watch release.

Do not use Cozy Watch marks in a product name, domain, social account, package publisher name, or download page if the use could cause users to believe the project is maintained, certified, or endorsed by Cozy Watch.

You may not remove copyright, license, or third-party notices from a distribution where those notices are required by their applicable licenses.

## Commercial use and sponsorship

Using the source code under the MIT License does not automatically grant a license to Cozy Watch trademarks. Commercial use of the marks, co-branding, sponsorship statements, or other uses beyond the permissions above require written permission.

Permission for one use does not create a general trademark license. Requests can be sent to [tiago@cozywatch.com](mailto:tiago@cozywatch.com) with the proposed wording, artwork, distribution channel, and duration.

## Enforcement

Cozy Watch may ask a project to change or stop a confusing use of the marks. Nothing in this policy limits rights granted by the MIT License or applicable law.
